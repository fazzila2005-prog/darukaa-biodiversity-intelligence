# Species Richness

## What is Species Richness?

Species richness is the number of different species found in a particular area.

For example, if an area contains 10 different bird species and another area contains 20 different bird species, the second area has higher bird species richness.

## Why is Species Richness important?

Species richness is a simple indicator of biodiversity.

Higher species richness can indicate that an area supports a wider variety of species.

## Environmental relationships

### Species Richness ↔ Habitat Diversity

Different habitats can provide different food, shelter and nesting opportunities.

Greater habitat diversity can therefore support a wider range of species.

### Species Richness ↔ Land Use

Changes in land use can alter or remove habitats.

Conversion of diverse habitats into simplified land uses can reduce the resources available to different species.

### Species Richness ↔ Water Availability

Water availability affects vegetation and other ecological resources.

Changes in water availability can therefore affect which species can survive in an area.

### Species Richness ↔ Climate

Temperature and rainfall influence the environmental conditions in which species can live.

Changes in climate conditions may alter habitat suitability for some species.

## Possible interventions

Depending on local conditions, possible approaches include:

- Increasing habitat diversity
- Maintaining native vegetation
- Protecting existing habitats
- Creating ecological corridors where appropriate
- Diversifying agricultural systems
- Reducing unnecessary habitat disturbance

## Important caution

Species richness alone does not describe all aspects of biodiversity.

It does not directly measure:

- Abundance of each species
- Genetic diversity
- Ecosystem function

The system should therefore consider species richness together with other environmental indicators when possible.

## Environmental metrics

Primary metric:
- Species Richness

Related metrics:
- Habitat Diversity
- Land Use
- Water Availability
- Temperature
- Rainfall
